Our Specializations

Comprehensive Orthodontic Services

We offer a full range of orthodontic treatments tailored to your unique needs.

Pediatric Orthodontics

Early evaluation and treatment for children. We monitor jaw growth and development, using interceptive techniques to guide teeth into proper alignment from a young age.

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces remain the most effective solution for complex orthodontic cases. Modern brackets are smaller, more comfortable, and work faster than ever before.

Ceramic Braces

Tooth-colored ceramic brackets offer a discreet alternative to metal braces. They blend naturally with your teeth while providing the same effective treatment results.

Clear Aligners

Nearly invisible removable aligners that straighten teeth without brackets or wires. Perfect for adults and teens who want a discreet treatment option with maximum comfort.

Orthodontic Splints

Custom-made splints for TMJ disorders, bruxism, and bite correction. We design precise appliances to relieve jaw pain and protect your teeth from grinding damage.

Lingual Braces

Braces placed behind the teeth for completely invisible treatment. Ideal for professionals and anyone who wants effective orthodontic care without visible appliances.

Retainers & Maintenance

Post-treatment retainers to maintain your new smile for life. We offer both fixed and removable options with regular follow-up care to ensure lasting results.

Digital Treatment Planning

3D scanning, digital X-rays, and computer-simulated treatment outcomes. See your future smile before treatment begins with our state-of-the-art imaging technology.

Jaw Correction

Comprehensive treatment for jaw misalignment and skeletal discrepancies. We work with oral surgeons to plan and execute complex cases for optimal functional and aesthetic outcomes.
